About Travel Finds

Independent travel media. No hype, no fake urgency.

What this site is

Travel Finds publishes practical guides about cheaper flights, city breaks, flexible dates and travel essentials. We focus on decisions travellers can actually use, not on inventing prices or pressure tactics.

How we make money

Some links are affiliate links. If you click one and book with a partner, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. This never changes the editorial rules: we do not invent live prices, fake discounts or availability.

Editorial rules

  • Sample prices and deal cards are clearly labelled as illustrative.
  • Affiliate links are marked with a sponsored link relationship.
  • We link to official sources for passenger rights, health and transport information where possible.
  • We correct material errors when they are found.

Corrections

If you spot an error, open an issue in the public repository. Please include the page URL and the exact statement that needs checking.

Contact

The best public contact channel is GitHub Issues. Do not send sensitive personal data.
