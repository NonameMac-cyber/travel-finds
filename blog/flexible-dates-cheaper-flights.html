Flexible Dates: How to Find Cheaper Flights

Why shifting your dates by a day or two often beats any promo code, how to search a whole month and when to stop looking for a cheaper fare.

Move your dates by a day or two — that's often enough to cut the fare by a third or more on popular routes. Search whole months, not single days.

Avoid Friday evenings and Sunday afternoons — business and leisure travellers both book then, pushing prices up. Tuesday–Thursday departures are usually cheaper.

Compare the whole trip — a cheap flight on a date with expensive hotels isn't a saving. Check accommodation for the same dates before celebrating.

How to search flexible dates

Open a calendar view on Kiwi.com or Google Flights and look at the price for every day of the month. Prices often drop midweek and spike before holidays.

If you're flexible on destination too, search by price first: pick the cheapest week, then decide where to go.

When to book

  • Peak season: 3–6 months ahead gives the most choice.
  • Low season: 3–8 weeks is often enough.
  • Connections: cheap combinations tend to appear later, four to eight weeks out.

If the fare fits your budget and the trip is confirmed, book it. Waiting for the absolute minimum is a gamble — prices move in both directions, and cheap seats sell out.

Two traps on cheap dates

The two-ticket connection. Separate cheap fares can look far cheaper than a single ticket — until a delay makes you miss the second flight. Book separate tickets only with three hours or more between them.

The hotel mismatch. Your flight is cheap because it's a quiet weekend — but that weekend may also mean sold-out hotels or a conference. Check accommodation prices for the exact same dates.

Alerts: useful if you use them right

Set an alert for your route, then forget it. When it triggers, check the actual dates and total price. An alert is a notification, not proof it's a good deal.

FAQ

Is it cheaper to fly on a Tuesday?

Not universally — it depends on the route and season. Compare actual dates rather than relying on a rule.

How far ahead should I search?

For peak dates, 3–6 months out. For low season, 3–8 weeks. Flexible dates help most when you still have options.

Do flexible dates work for return trips?

Yes — shift the return first, since that's usually the expensive half.

What if my dates are fixed?

Flexibility moves to airports, connection length, time of day, and how far ahead you book. Compare several real options before paying.
