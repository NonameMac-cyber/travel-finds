Cookie Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

This page explains the storage and cookies used by Travel Finds and by the partner services connected to it.

What we store

We do not use first-party advertising cookies. We store your cookie choice in your browser's local storage under the key tf-consent-v1. This is essential to remember your choice.

Cloudflare Web Analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page views and understand aggregate traffic. It is cookieless, does not create a cross-site profile and does not identify individual visitors.

Affiliate cookies

TravelPayouts and travel partners may set cookies when you use the flight search widget or click a partner link. These cookies help attribute a booking to this site and usually last up to 30 days. They are controlled by the partner and are subject to the partner's own cookie and privacy policies.

Flight search widget

The TravelPayouts flight search widget is loaded only after you choose Accept all. If you choose Essential only, we show direct partner links instead. The widget may set cookies or local storage when it loads.

Managing cookies

Use the Cookie settings link in the footer to reset your choice, or clear site data in your browser. Blocking partner cookies may affect search and booking features.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the public GitHub issue tracker linked in the footer.
