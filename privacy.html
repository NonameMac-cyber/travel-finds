Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Travel Finds is an independent travel media project. It is not an airline, hotel, travel agency or financial adviser.

Who is responsible

Travel Finds is responsible for the editorial content on this website. You can contact us through the public GitHub issue tracker linked at the bottom of this page.

What we process

  • Technical data from our hosting providers: IP address, browser type, requested page, time and status code. This is needed to deliver and secure the site.
  • Aggregate analytics: page views, referrers, country and device type through Cloudflare Web Analytics. It is cookieless and does not create a cross-site profile of you.
  • Affiliate attribution: when you click a partner link or use the flight search widget, TravelPayouts or the travel partner may set cookies and record a referral. See our Cookie Policy.
  • Messages you send us voluntarily through GitHub. Please do not include sensitive personal data.

Why we use it

To run the site, understand which guides are useful, measure affiliate referrals, prevent abuse and answer messages.

Legal bases

Where GDPR applies, we rely on legitimate interests for hosting, security and aggregate analytics; consent where required for partner cookies; and the steps you request when you contact us.

Third parties

Our hosting and infrastructure providers include GitHub Pages, Vercel and Cloudflare. Affiliate services include TravelPayouts and its travel partners. We do not sell personal data. Partner websites are governed by their own privacy policies.

Retention

Hosting logs are kept according to each provider's retention settings. Analytics data is aggregate. Messages posted in GitHub remain public unless you contact us through another channel.

Your rights

You may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection or portability where applicable. You may also complain to your local data protection authority. To exercise a right, contact us through GitHub Issues.

Changes

We will update this page when our practices change. The date at the top shows the latest revision.
